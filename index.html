What is the plan for each severity?

Triage + remediate takes a severity to zero. Triage only pays to disposition the findings, so the false positives leave and the real ones stay on the books by choice. Not in plan costs nothing and moves nothing.

Credit rates

Every finding is triaged. Only the true positives are remediated — false positives cost one triage credit and stop there.
